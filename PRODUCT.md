# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS, following the supplied reference repository's no-build preview pattern.

## Users

Designers, marketers, developers, and AI coding agents producing Pipeline Gurus social and digital brand work.

## Product Purpose

Provide one portable, implementation-ready source of truth for Pipeline Gurus typography, color, composition, assets, templates, and voice.

## Operating Context

The system supports social carousels, single posts, stories, sales education, promotional calls to action, and related web or presentation surfaces.

## Brand Commitments

Pipeline Gurus uses an elite, fast, sharp, bold, intelligent visual identity. The source workbook explicitly anchors the feel in high-performance automotive precision and financial authority. Bebas Neue, Poppins, black, white, and `#F6283F` are fixed commitments.

## Evidence on Hand

`PG Social Media Guidelines.xlsx` contains 18 template definitions, 26 composition rules, and the official Pipeline Gurus logo asset. No testimonials, performance claims, customer logos, or case-study results were supplied and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make one message visually dominant.
- Use negative space to signal confidence.
- Reserve red for action or emphasis.
- Keep every composition aligned, flat, and precise.
- Prefer specific sales intelligence over motivational language.
