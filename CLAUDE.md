# Pipeline Gurus Design Language — agent instructions

Treat `DESIGN.md` as the visual source of truth and `tokens/` as the machine-readable implementation source.

Use the official logo at `assets/pipeline-gurus-logo.jpg`. Do not generate, trace, recolor, distort, crop, or replace it. The supplied asset is white on black; use it on black or in contexts where its full rectangular lockup remains intact.

Never invent colors, font families, soft shapes, gradient effects, or additional logo variants. Headings use Bebas Neue. Body and UI use Poppins. The only action accent is `#F6283F`.

Social compositions must preserve the source workbook rules: a 12-column grid, 40–50% negative space, one hero, one logo, one CTA maximum, and left-weighted or Z-pattern balance.
